Benefit. Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) gives financial assistance to rural families for building a pucca house. The unit assistance is Rs. 1.20 lakh in plain areas and Rs. 1.30 lakh in hilly states, difficult areas and Integrated Action Plan districts. The house must be at least 25 square metres in size and include a hygienic cooking space.

Additional support. Beneficiaries are entitled to 90 or 95 person-days of unskilled labour under MGNREGS, and up to Rs. 12,000 for a toilet through convergence with the Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin.

Eligibility. Rural households that are houseless or living in zero, one or two room kutcha houses with kutcha walls and roof. Beneficiaries are selected using Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data and the list is verified by the Gram Sabha.

Documents required. Aadhaar, job card number if available, bank account details, and the SBM-G toilet registration number.

How to apply. Beneficiaries are identified from the verified list, so there is no open application. Contact the Gram Panchayat or block office to check whether your household is on the Awaas+ or SECC permanent wait list.
