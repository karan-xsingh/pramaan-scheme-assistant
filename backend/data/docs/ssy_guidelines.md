Benefit. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a small savings scheme for the girl child. It offers a fixed government-notified interest rate, revised every quarter, and tax benefits on deposits, interest and maturity.

Eligibility. A parent or legal guardian can open an account in the name of a girl child below 10 years of age. One account per girl child, and at most two accounts per family, with an exception for twins or triplets.

Deposits. The minimum deposit is Rs. 250 in a financial year and the maximum is Rs. 1.5 lakh per year. Deposits are made for 15 years from the date of opening.

Maturity. The account matures 21 years after it is opened. Partial withdrawal of up to 50 percent is allowed after the girl turns 18 for higher education or marriage.

Documents required. Birth certificate of the girl child, identity and address proof of the parent or guardian.

How to apply. Open the account at any post office or authorised bank branch with the form and documents.
