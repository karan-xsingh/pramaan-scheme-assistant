Benefit. Under PM-KISAN, eligible landholding farmer families receive income support of Rs. 6,000 per year. The amount is paid in three equal instalments of Rs. 2,000 every four months, directly into the beneficiary's bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Eligibility. The scheme covers farmer families who own cultivable land according to land records. The family is defined as husband, wife and minor children.

Exclusions. The following categories are not eligible: institutional landholders; families holding or having held constitutional posts; serving or retired officers and employees of central or state governments; retired pensioners drawing Rs. 10,000 or more per month; people who paid income tax in the last assessment year; and professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers and chartered accountants registered with professional bodies.

Documents required. Aadhaar number, land ownership records, and a bank account number linked for DBT. Mobile number is needed for OTP verification.

How to apply. A farmer can self-register on the PM-KISAN portal using the Farmers Corner, or register through a Common Service Centre (CSC), or through the local patwari or state nodal officer. e-KYC must be completed to continue receiving instalments.
