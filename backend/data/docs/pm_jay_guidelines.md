Benefit. Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides health cover of up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. Treatment is cashless and paperless at public hospitals and empanelled private hospitals. There is no cap on family size or age of members.

Eligibility. Families are identified using deprivation and occupational criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 for rural and urban areas. Senior citizens aged 70 years and above are also covered under an extension of the scheme, irrespective of income.

Documents required. Aadhaar card or another government photo ID, and ration card or proof of family membership. An Ayushman card is generated after verification.

How to apply. Check eligibility on the official beneficiary portal or by calling the helpline 14555. Visit an empanelled hospital's Ayushman Mitra desk or a Common Service Centre for verification and to get the Ayushman card. Registration is free of charge.
