Benefit. Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a pension scheme mainly for workers in the unorganised sector. It gives a guaranteed minimum monthly pension of Rs. 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 4,000 or 5,000 after the subscriber reaches the age of 60 years. The amount depends on the contribution and the age at which the subscriber joins.

Eligibility. Any citizen of India aged between 18 and 40 years with a savings bank account or post office savings account can join. Since 1 October 2022, income tax payers are not eligible to join APY.

Contribution. The subscriber chooses a pension amount. The monthly, quarterly or half-yearly contribution is auto-debited from the bank account. The earlier a person joins, the lower the contribution.

Documents required. Aadhaar (preferred), mobile number and a savings bank account.

How to apply. Visit the bank branch or post office where you hold the account, fill the APY registration form and give consent for auto-debit. Net banking and mobile banking can also be used where available.
