Benefit. Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) provides deposit-free LPG connections to women from poor households. Under the original scheme the Government provided financial support of Rs. 1,600 per connection to cover the security deposit, pressure regulator, booklet and safety hose.

Eligibility. The applicant must be an adult woman (18 years or above) from a poor household, such as BPL families or other listed categories like SC/ST households, Antyodaya Anna Yojana beneficiaries and tea garden workers. There must be no existing LPG connection in the household.

Documents required. A filled application form with KYC, Aadhaar, ration card or other proof of the family's poor status, and a bank account number.

How to apply. Submit the application form with documents at the nearest LPG distributor, or apply online on the PMUY portal. The connection is released in the name of the woman of the household.
